# Website content and visitor scope

The redesign will prioritize newcomers planning a visit and people looking to connect with a Missional Community, while retaining contact and serving paths. It will keep WordPress publishing for staff and volunteers, preserve existing articles and newsletters with dates and authors where available, provide separate areas for articles, newsletters, and sermons, and show sermon thumbnails that link to YouTube rather than embedded players. Newsletters should be readable online and downloadable where source content permits. The launch scope includes the homepage, visit/contact information, Missional Communities, articles, newsletters, and YouTube sermon thumbnails, with a warm, welcoming, mobile-first design. The target is a mobile performance score above 88%.
