# GracePoint Church Waiyaki Way Website

This glossary defines the church-specific content and visitor journeys used in the website redesign.

## Language

**Visitor**:
A person exploring the church website, including someone considering attending a service or connecting with a community.

**Plan a visit**:
The information and actions that help someone attend in person, including service time, location, directions, and what to expect.

**Missional Community**:
A local or online family of believers who meet regularly for discipleship, evangelism, encouragement, and prayer.
_Avoid_: MC (in visitor-facing copy)

**Article**:
A written piece published by the church, such as a reflection, teaching, or ministry update.

**Newsletter**:
A periodic church publication containing news and updates, available to read online and, where provided, download.

**Sermon**:
A church message published as a YouTube video and presented on the website through its thumbnail and link to YouTube.
_Avoid_: Embedded sermon (for the agreed website presentation)

**Serving opportunity**:
A way for someone to participate in the church’s ministries and serve others.
